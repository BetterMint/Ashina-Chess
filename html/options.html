FLAG MODE
When your clock drops below 10 or 5 seconds, auto move delay, personality and depth are adjusted internally to maximize flagging chances.
CENTER WEIGHT
Controls how auto move delays are distributed within your min–max range. Higher values cluster timing toward the midpoint using a bell curve, making your moves feel more natural.
DRAGON PERSONALITIES
Personalities other than Default will internally set parameters to reflect that playing style. To use the engine at maximum strength, set the personality to Default.
AGGRESSIVE: The Aggressive personality will attack relentlessly with no concern for their own safety. They prefer active pieces, and are biased toward playing with the Queen. DEFENSIVE: The Defensive personality emphasizes king safety and a solid position above all else. ACTIVE: The Active personality tends toward open positions and well-placed pieces. POSITIONAL: The Positional personality tends toward solid play, maneuvering, and more closed positions. ENDGAME: The Endgame personality prefers playing through to the end to win by promoting a pawn. BEGINNER: The Beginner personality doesn't understand the fundamentals of chess, especially endgame principles, and is mostly looking to check and capture. HUMAN: The Human personality plays aggressively, avoiding pointless simplification and repetition, aiming for open positions, and emphasizing static advantages.
RODENT PERSONALITIES
FISCHER: Attacking, contemptuous, raised mobility for both sides. ANAND: Fast player, slight preference for attack, cares for pawn structure. RETI: Disregards classical piece placement, solid pawn structure. BOTVINNIK: Balanced, cares for pawn structure, somewhat willing to accept doubled pawns. MARSHALL: Very high attack and mobility, sacrificial, favors knights. LARSEN: Tricky player, follows Nimzowitsch principles, plays varied and unusual openings. SPASSKY: Defensive player who likes grabbing space and cares for pawn structure. PETROSIAN: Defensive, prefers closed positions, likes sacrificing exchange. TARRASCH: Mobility-focused, emphasizes bishops, likes open games. NIMZOWITSCH: Hypermodern chess, preferred to control it from a distance with pieces. RUBINSTEIN: Classical player, marginally prefers defense, decreased mobility, favors rook play, steers towards endgame. ANDERSSEN: Aggressive, king-hunting attacks and spectacular piece sacrifices. STEINITZ: Defensive, accepts cramped positions and sacrifices, solid with pawns. KINGHUNTER: Mad attacker. TORTOISE: Slow defensive player who likes blocked positions. DRUNK: Huge random factor in evaluation.
